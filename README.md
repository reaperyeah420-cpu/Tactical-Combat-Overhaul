# Tactical Combat Overhaul

A Starsector combat overhaul: command your fleet like an RTS, and fight with grounded, tactical rules.

## Features

- **RTS fleet command**: select ships, give orders, formations, stances and control groups, or jump into any ship and fly it yourself.
- **Fog of war**: you only see what your ships can see and radar detects threats before you can see them.
- **Bigger battlefields** with new objectives: the Supply FOB and the Repair Dock.
- **Armor that behaves like armor**: penetration, ricochets, magazine cook-offs and fires.
- **Limited ammo** for ballistic guns, refilled by supply ships and their shuttles.
- **Reactor power** alongside flux, with meltdowns from deep hits.
- **Radar and IR missiles**: radar tracking, datalinks, ECM and noise jamming; heat-seekers and flares.
- **Boarding**: send marines to capture ships deck by deck, with orders, experience and surprises along the way.
- **Ships die slowly**: they burn and break up while their crew escape in pods you can rescue.
- **Mid-battle logistics**: repair tenders, tugs that tow stranded ships, and new hullmods for all of it.
- **NPC fleets play by the same rules**, with their own support ships and TCO hullmods.

Built to work with ship and weapon mods: their weapons, ships and missiles are sorted into TCO's rules automatically.
Almost everything can be tweaked, disabled or changed in one way or another, built so you can customize the way you want to play.

## Requirements

- Starsector 0.98a-RC8
- LunaLib (optional, recommended): in-game settings menu

## Installation

Download the latest zip from [Releases](../../releases), unzip it into your Starsector `mods` folder and enable it in the launcher. Start a new game or load a save.

Removing TCO from a save it has been used in is not supported.

## Settings

With LunaLib, every major system can be tuned or switched off from the in-game mod settings. Without it, edit `data/config/tco_settings.json`.
