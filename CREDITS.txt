Tactical Combat Overhaul - credits and third-party material
============================================================

GraphicsLib, by Harrison Snodgrass (DarkRevenant)
  The look of TCO's shipboard fires (tco.armor.Fires) is adapted from GraphicsLib's
  burning-wreck flames (org.dark.graphics.plugins.ShipDestructionEffects): the particle recipe,
  its sizes, timings and colour blending, and its use of each hull style's explosion colour.
  Changes made: the effect is rewritten for TCO's own fires, pinned to a point on the hull,
  with its own fire size and fade-out, and it reads the colours itself so GraphicsLib is not
  required.

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Thanks to qcwxezda (Unthemed Weapons Collection), whose refit-screen weapon numbers inspired
TCO's ship-adjusted weapon cards. No code or assets from it are used.
